Core Home Inspection Service

A comprehensive, non-invasive visual examination of the physical structure and vital mechanical systems of the home. Backed by InterNACHI Standards of Practice, Tom Burkett provides the methodical clarity and insight you need before buying or selling in Saguache County, Crestone, Moffat, Alamosa, and the Salida area, Colorado.

🏠

Roofing & Attic

Thorough assessment of roof coverings, flashing, chimneys, skylights, gutters, drainage, roof framing, attic insulation depth, and proper attic ventilation to prevent heat loss and ice damming.

🧱

Foundation & Structure

Inspection of foundation walls, crawl spaces, concrete slabs, floor framing, load-bearing walls, posts, and beams for settlement, water seepage, shifting, or structural deterioration.

⚡

Electrical Systems

Examination of the main electrical service panel, breakers and fuses, service entrance conductors, wiring conditions, grounding, and testing of GFCI/AFCI safety devices throughout the home.

🚰

Plumbing Systems

Testing interior water supply lines, water pressure, drainage, drain/waste/vent piping, shut-off valves, water heating equipment, functional flow across all fixtures, and active leak detection.

🔥

Heating & Cooling (HVAC)

Evaluation of furnaces, boilers, heat pumps, thermostats, ductwork, filters, and safety controls—critical for dependable operation during freezing Colorado winters and high altitude.

🚪

Exterior & Grounds

Evaluation of siding, trim, eaves, exterior doors, windows, decks, porches, railings, stairs, walkways, driveways, retaining walls, and soil grading for positive drainage away from the home.

🪟

Interior & Living Spaces

Inspection of ceilings, interior walls, floors, doors, stairways, handrails, egress compliance, fireplaces, and installed built-in kitchen appliances.

🔍

Thermal & Moisture Screening

Advanced visual detection for draft pathways, thermal bridging, hidden moisture accumulation, and insulation gaps that cause high utility bills in Southern Colorado's alpine climate.

📋

Fast Digital Delivery

Easy-to-read, comprehensive report delivered within 24 hours complete with high-resolution photos, concise descriptions, and actionable summaries.

🤝
